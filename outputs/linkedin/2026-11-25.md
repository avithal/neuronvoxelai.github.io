Gemini 4 Carbon just topped the auto-annotation leaderboard. It's also not the model you should buy.

Ultralytics quietly dropped a VLM benchmark snapshot on Nov 11 — 45 models ranked on UL37, a 36-dataset object-detection/auto-annotation suite. Gemini 4 Carbon wins at 0.584 mAP50. GPT-6.1 Terra lands at roughly 96% of that accuracy for about a third of the cost. And buried under the leaderboard: a flat $1.00 per 1,000 images just for the batch-annotation processing fee, on top of whatever the model itself charges per token.

Nobody's done the arithmetic that actually matters here:

→ "Best mAP" and "best $/useful label" are two different leaderboards, and almost every team buys off the wrong one.
→ A 4% accuracy gap sounds trivial until you multiply it by how many of those extra "correct" labels you were going to manually re-check anyway.
→ The real cost isn't the model call. It's the model call plus the QA pass plus the relabel rate — and vendors only ever publish the first number.

This is the same mistake teams made with edge chip spec sheets (TOPS ≠ real throughput), just moved one layer up the stack: a clean leaderboard number that quietly assumes your use case, your error tolerance, and your budget don't exist.
